Vedic Maths · Coding · Creative Thinking — 48 sessions across two years

Two books. One journey. From first sprite to first game.

Each workbook contains 24 weekly 35-minute sessions designed to be taught in any school computer lab. Every session blends a Vedic Maths warm-up, a coding concept, a guided build, and a moment to share — so children leave the room as creators, not just consumers.

How Every Session Works

Workbook Guide · Page 02
Both workbooks follow the same predictable rhythm. Once a teacher delivers it three times, it runs itself. Once a child experiences it three times, they arrive ready to think.
8 min
Step 1

Vedic Warm-Up

Five mental-maths problems, no calculators, using the session's sutra. Builds speed, focus and confidence before the screens come on.

5 min
Step 2

Sutra ↔ Code Bridge

Teacher draws the explicit link: "the rule you just used by hand is exactly what these blocks do on screen." Students record it on their worksheet.

15 min
Step 3

Build / Code Time

Students open the day's Scratch starter file and follow the guided build. Fast finishers tackle the extension challenge printed at the bottom of the page.

7 min
Step 4

Share & Reflect

Two students show their screen. Class discusses. Everyone writes one sentence: "Today I connected ___ to ___."

The Skill Ladder

How concepts build across both books
Each rung of the ladder builds directly on the last. Nothing is taught twice from scratch — every Grade 5 concept stands on a Grade 4 foundation. This is what makes the program spiral instead of repetitive.
Rung 1 · Book 1, Sessions 1–8

Express & Move

  • Stage, sprites, costumes
  • "When flag clicked", say, wait
  • Motion: glide, turn, x/y
  • Pick random
  • First variable (set / change)
  • Doubling & halving (Vedic)
Rung 2 · Book 1, Sessions 9–24

Repeat & Ask

  • "repeat" and "forever" loops
  • Ask & answer (input)
  • Score variables
  • Multiple sprites & backdrops
  • Broadcast (introduction only)
  • Ekadhikena, Nikhilam-from-100, ×9, ×11 (Vedic)
Rung 3 · Book 2, Sessions 1–12

Decide & Loop Deeper

  • All operators incl. modulo
  • if / else & comparisons
  • Logical operators (and / or / not)
  • Nested loops
  • Two-player input
  • Full Nikhilam & Urdhva multiplication (Vedic)
Rung 4 · Book 2, Sessions 13–24

Abstract & Ship

  • Lists (item of, length of, random)
  • Broadcast / receive (full)
  • Custom blocks (functions w/ params)
  • Debugging methodology
  • Project-based final build
  • Anurupyena, digital-root verification (Vedic)

Difficulty Curve at a Glance

Both books deliberately start gently and ramp into harder territory in the final term. By Session 24 of Book 2, students are working at a level normally seen in Grade 7 worksheets — but they reach it without ever feeling stuck, because every concept was introduced two sessions before it was needed.

The 60 / 40 Promise

Across the two books, roughly 25 of the 48 sessions are explicitly Vedic-themed (52%) and 16 are pure-fun creative builds (33%) — with the remaining 7 reserved for mini-projects and showcases. This intentional balance keeps Vedic Maths central without ever turning Scratch class into "another maths period". Children come for the games and leave fluent in sutras.

What's Next After Book 2?

A child who finishes both Scratch workbooks is ready for the Grades 6–7 track: real web programming with HTML, CSS and JavaScript — where the same sutras reappear as functions, for loops, and DOM manipulation.

From Scratch blocks to real code, the journey stays the same: think the sutra, write the code, ship the project.
